Privacy Policy

Goal In app · Effective November 19, 2026 · 한국어

Summary: Goal In has no sign-up or login, and the developer runs no servers. Your goals, entries and photos are stored only on your phone; the developer never receives or sees them. The app has no ads and no analytics. Only the connections you turn on yourself (external calendars, fitness apps) work, within the limits described below.

1. Information stored on your phone

  • Goals, entries (date, time, amounts, notes), category and field settings, items such as books or subjects, and app settings that you enter
  • Photos you take with the camera or pick from your gallery (entry photos, goal icons)

This information stays in the app's storage and is not sent to the developer. It may also be included in backup files you create and in your phone's automatic backup (section 4).

2. Health data (Health Connect · Apple Health)

Only if you turn on Settings → Fitness app connection, the app reads the following from your phone's health store (Health Connect on Android, Apple Health on iPhone). It never writes to or changes the health store.

  • Workouts (type, start and end time), workout distance, step counts
  • Calories burned: requested on Android because it is needed to read workout details, but never stored or used by the app.
  • Background reading (Android): only if you allow it, the app reads this data without opening when you tap the refresh button on the home screen widget.

How it is used: solely to create entries from your workouts and steps and count them toward your goals. These entries are stored on your phone like any other entry, and are also included in backup files you create and in your phone's automatic backup (your own account, section 4).

Retention: so the same workout is never imported twice, the app keeps the type, start and end time, and the health app's record ID of each imported workout and daily step count. This is kept while the entry exists (and is included in backup files); if you delete the entry, it is deleted after the entry is permanently removed from the Bin (after 30 days, at the latest once that workout falls outside the period the app reads). Disconnecting keeps entries already imported and this information; uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Health data is not sent to the developer or anyone else, is never sold, and is never used for advertising, marketing or credit decisions. If you turn on an external calendar connection, entries created from workouts are sent to that calendar as events (a text summary), just like other entries.

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time in your phone settings (Health Connect, or iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access) and disconnect in the app under Settings → Fitness app connection.

3. External calendar connections (optional)

Only if you connect a calendar, the app sends your entries to it as all-day events (title and description = entry summary, the names of goals the entry counts toward, book or subject names, and the note), and creates, updates and deletes those events. The data goes directly from your device to the calendar service, never through the developer.

  • Naver Calendar: your Naver ID and app password are kept only in the phone's secure storage and are used only to talk to Naver's CalDAV server (caldav.calendar.naver.com).
  • Google Calendar: with Google Sign-In, the app gets permission to create, update and delete calendar events and to read your calendar list. The app never receives your Google password and does not store access tokens itself (your sign-in state is managed by the phone's Google Sign-In); only your account email and the chosen calendar are kept in secure storage. The app does not read your other events. Goal In's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Events sent to these services are governed by their own privacy policies. Disconnecting in Settings stops sending (events already sent can be deleted in each calendar).

4. Backups

  • Backup files: when you create a backup, the file is saved where you choose (a file location, or an app you pick from the share sheet). It is never sent to the developer.
  • Your phone's automatic backup: if Android's Google backup (or iCloud backup on iPhone) is on, the operating system may back up the app's entry data to your account. On Android, secure storage containing passwords and the photo folder are excluded from cloud backup. On iPhone, connection details (Naver ID and app password, Google account email) are kept in the Keychain readable on this device only, so they are not included in iPhone backups or transfers to a new phone. You can turn automatic backup off in your phone settings.

5. Camera, photos and notifications

  • Camera and photo access is used only when you add a photo or save a share card image. Only the photos you choose are copied into the app.
  • Notifications (entry, goal and backup reminders, the lock screen notification, etc.) are created on your phone without any server.

6. Send feedback

Settings → Send feedback opens your mail app. Only if you actually send the email does the developer receive your email address, what you wrote, and the app version, device model, OS version and language pre-filled in the body (you may delete the device details before sending). This is used only to reply and fix problems, kept for one year after the reply is finished, and then deleted from the mailbox. The feedback mailbox is a Gmail account, so these emails are stored on the servers of Google LLC (USA).

7. Payments

If you buy Premium (subscription or lifetime purchase), payment is processed by Google Play or the App Store. The developer never receives card or payment details; the app only checks the purchase status reported by the store.

8. Sharing and advertising

Apart from feedback emails (section 6), the developer receives no personal information, and feedback emails are never shared with or sold to third parties. The app uses no ads, no advertising ID, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

9. Deleting your information and your rights

  • You can view, edit and delete the information on your phone in the app. Deleted entries and goals are permanently removed from the Bin after 30 days (along with the workout import details in section 2).
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its data on the phone. However, if Android automatic backup is on, entries may be restored when you reinstall. On iPhone, connection details may remain in the Keychain on the phone after uninstalling and are deleted the first time you open the app after reinstalling; to avoid this, disconnect calendars in Settings before uninstalling.
  • Events sent to external calendars and backup files you created must be deleted where they are stored.
  • You can ask to access, correct, delete or stop processing your feedback emails by contacting us below.

10. Security

  • Entries are stored only on your phone, with no server.
  • Calendar connection details are encrypted in the operating system's secure storage (Android Keystore, iPhone Keychain).
  • Communication with external services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) only.
  • The feedback mailbox is protected with two-step verification.

11. Children

Goal In is a general-audience tracking app, and the developer does not ask for age.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and significant changes will be announced in app update notes.
